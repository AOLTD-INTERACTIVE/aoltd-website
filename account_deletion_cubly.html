🗂️ What Data Gets Deleted

✅ Permanently Removed from CUBLY:

  • Game progress and save data
  • High scores and leaderboard entries
  • In-game purchases and virtual currency
  • Display name and profile settings
  • Account credentials (email/username/password if registered)
  • Device association data
  • Profile background and avatar choices

📝 What We Keep (by legal requirement):

  • Transaction records for tax/audit purposes (anonymized)
  • Aggregated analytics (non-personally identifiable)

⏱️ Timeline

Once you email us with the subject "Account Deletion Requests-AOLTD: CUBLY":

  • Within 48 hours: We'll reply to confirm you want to proceed
  • After your confirmation: Your account will be deleted within 30 days (as required by GDPR and Google Play policies)
  • Completion notification: We'll email you when deletion is complete

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What if I don't know my PlayFab ID?

That's okay! Just send us the email address associated with your account or describe your account as best you can. We'll work with you to identify the correct account before deletion.

Can I delete my account from within the game?

Not yet. For now, email requests are the only way. We're working on adding an in-app deletion option in a future update.

How long does it take?

We process all deletion requests within 30 days of your confirmation, as required by data protection laws. Most requests are completed within 7-14 days.

Can I recover my account after deletion?

No. Deletion is permanent. If you reinstall CUBLY, a brand new account will be created.

What about GDPR/California privacy rights?

We comply with all applicable data protection regulations. This deletion process fulfills your "right to be forgotten" under GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws.

Will this affect my accounts in other AOLTD games?

Currently, deletion requests are per game. If you play multiple AOLTD games, you'll need to submit separate requests for each game. We're working on a unified account system for the future.
